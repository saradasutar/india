# Central Government Employee Toolkit — calculation notes

This is an independent estimation aid, not a Government of India service or an official pay/pension determination. The applicable service rules, notifications, department orders, PPO, PAO and competent authority prevail.

## 7th CPC promotion / MACP pay fixation

The tool applies the general method in Rule 13 of the CCS (Revised Pay) Rules, 2016: one increment in the existing level, then placement at the equal or next higher cell in the promoted level. It uses the standard civilian pay matrix starting cells and generates subsequent stages by 3% progression rounded to the nearest ₹100, with matrix cell limits by level. It does not cover special cadre matrices, NPA, personal pay, stepping-up, option for DNI, or later clarifications. Validate any case against the official matrix and Rule 13 before using it for an order.

Official source: https://doe.gov.in/files/cenetral-pay_document/7thCPC_revisedpayrules25072016.pdf

## HRA and transport allowance

Both tools require the user to enter the applicable rates. HRA is estimated as basic pay × entered HRA percentage. Transport allowance is entered as the notified monthly slab and DA is calculated on that allowance. The tools do not infer city category, DA threshold, eligibility, double-rate entitlement, absence or government accommodation.

Official order directory: https://doe.gov.in/en/order-central-pay-commission/16

## Retirement gratuity

The estimator uses (basic pay + DA) × 1/4 × completed six-monthly qualifying-service periods, capped at 66 periods and ₹25,00,000. The ₹25 lakh ceiling was made effective from 1 January 2024. Actual emoluments, qualifying service, scheme coverage and admissibility must be verified by the department/PAO.

Official references:
- CCS Pension Rules, 2021: https://doppw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Amendment_CCS_PensionRule-Eng.pdf
- DoPPW / PIB gratuity ceiling order: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2022470

## Retirement leave encashment

The estimator calculates the EL component as (pay + DA) × EL days / 30, and the HPL component as (half-pay leave salary + DA on that leave salary) × HPL days / 30. Combined EL and HPL must not exceed 300 days. The leave account, leave salary, retirement type and rule-specific conditions must be checked by the office.

Reference: CCS (Leave) Rules, 1972, Rule 39(2), current compilation: https://dopt.gov.in/

## Pension commutation

The estimator calculates the commuted monthly amount up to 40% of basic pension and the lump sum as commuted monthly amount × 12 × user-entered official commutation factor. The user must obtain the correct factor from the applicable commutation table using age next birthday. It does not determine eligibility, medical examination, effective date or restoration date.

Official reference: https://cpao.nic.in/Pensioner/pensioners.php

## Basic pension

The basic pension estimator applies 50% of the higher of emoluments or average emoluments, subject to the ₹9,000 minimum and ₹1,25,000 maximum stated in Rule 44. The tool is a simplified estimate and does not cover invalid-pension exceptions, compulsory retirement, compassionate allowance, family pension, NPS/UPS, additional pension or other special provisions.

Official reference: https://doppw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Amendment_CCS_PensionRule-Eng.pdf

## Quality and limitations

- All calculator inputs are processed in the browser; no calculator input is sent to a backend.
- Examples are illustrative and must not be used as current allowance rates.
- A calculation is not a sanction, pay-fixation order, PPO, tax determination or legal opinion.
- Before publishing additional government calculators, verify current rules and amendments from the issuing department and retain a dated source note.
