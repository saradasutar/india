How do I add holidays to Google Calendar?

Use a computer browser: in Google Calendar, choose Other calendars → Add → From URL, then paste the published calendar URL. Some links open a shared calendar directly; a raw iCal URL is offered here as a copy button. The owner must make the holiday calendar public. A PDF alone does not add holidays to your calendar.
